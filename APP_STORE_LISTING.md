## 3. App Privacy questionnaire

Unchanged by the second side: one account, same data. App Store Connect → **App Privacy** → **Get Started**.

**"Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?"** → **Yes.**

Then tick these data types. For every one: **Used for tracking: No.** We share nothing with advertisers or data brokers, so the app does not need the "Allow tracking?" prompt.

| Data type (Apple's name) | What it is for us | Linked to the user? | Purpose(s) to tick |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Contact Info → Email Address** | Sign-in, account emails, alert emails | **Yes** | App Functionality |
| **Identifiers → User ID** | The account ID that holds saved searches, watchlist and alerts | **Yes** | App Functionality, Analytics |
| **Identifiers → Device ID** | The push notification token Apple gives the device, so we can send alerts | **Yes** | App Functionality |
| **Usage Data → Product Interaction** | Funnel steps (visited, searched, opened a card, starred, signed up), plus page-view counts | **Yes** (see note) | Analytics |
| **Search History** | The words and filters in searches a member **saves**, so we can alert them | **Yes** | App Functionality |
| **User Content → Other User Content** | The watchlist (starred listings), bid reminder settings, and (if it ships) "I own this" marks on card checklists | **Yes** | App Functionality |

**Do not tick:** Purchases, Financial Info, Location, Contacts, Health, Photos, Name, Phone Number, Physical Address, Browsing History, Advertising Data, Sensitive Info.

**Purchases → none for now.** When paid plans go on, add **Purchases → Purchase History**, linked, App Functionality.

### Things to know (flagged)

1. **Product Interaction: we chose "linked", the cautious answer.** Most funnel events are anonymous (a random ID on the device, no name, email or search words). But "signed up" and "started a trial" are stored with the account ID, so "linked" is the honest answer.
2. **Search History and Other User Content** are declared because the app stores saved search words, the watchlist and owned-card marks against the account. One-off searches that aren't saved are sent to eBay to get results and not kept with the account.
3. **Push token as "Device ID".** Apple has no category made for push tokens. Device ID is the cautious choice.
4. **Server logs.** Hosting (Vercel, Supabase) keeps short-lived technical logs with IP addresses, for security and fixing errors. Not declared. To be fully cautious, add **Diagnostics → Other Diagnostic Data**, not linked, App Functionality.
5. **RevenueCat is built into the app but switched off** (its key isn't set during early access), so it collects nothing. Update the label when it's switched on.
6. **Name.** The app doesn't ask for or store a name. Google sign-in (which shares a name and photo) is hidden in the app.
7. **If Foils & Fables adds any new data** (for example a separate analytics tool on its own domain), this section must be updated before submission.

## 6. What a reviewer might object to, and our answer

**Guideline 5.2.1 / 2.3.7, Game trademarks (highest risk for the two-sided app).**
Our answer: no game, character or card maker names or logos in the app name, subtitle, keywords, description, icon or screenshot captions. Inside the app, game names appear only as plain-text filter labels describing what is for sale, next to a "not affiliated with any game publisher" line. Card pictures are eBay sellers' own photos of the cards they are selling, not artwork we supply. Screenshots should favour cards where no game logo is the main thing in frame (see the screenshots README). If Apple still asks, we can show that we use eBay's official API and that the names only describe listings.

**Guideline 4.2, Minimum functionality ("this is just a website").**
Our answer: native push alerts for saved searches and bid reminders, Sign in with Apple, a native side chooser, switch and tab bar, and account deletion. The alerts are the main reason to have the app. If Apple still pushes back, the next step is more native features (for example a home screen widget of watched auctions), not arguing.

**Guideline 5.2.1 / 5.2.2, Using eBay's name and content.**
Our answer: we use eBay's official Browse API under eBay's developer terms, we say clearly that we are not affiliated with eBay, and we don't use eBay's logo or put "eBay" in the app name, subtitle or keywords.

**Guideline 3.1.1, In-app purchase.**
Our answer: nothing is sold in the app. Cards are physical goods bought from sellers on eBay, outside the app (guideline 3.1.3(e), goods used outside the app). No paid plans in this version.

**Guideline 4.3, Spam / one app per purpose.**
Our answer: one app, two sides, one account. That is the reason for combining them rather than submitting two near-identical apps.

**Guideline 5.1.1(v), Account deletion.**
Our answer: Account tab → Delete account, inside the app.

**Guideline 4.8, Sign in with Apple.**
Our answer: offered. Google sign-in is hidden in the app.

**Guideline 2.1, App completeness / demo account.**
Our answer: the demo account has saved searches and starred cards on both sides.

**Guideline 1.5 / 2.3, Accurate metadata.**
Our answer: screenshots are of the real app with real current eBay listings. Nothing is mocked up.
