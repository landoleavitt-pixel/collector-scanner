Two sides, one family

The sister side keeps F&F's skeleton: the same layout, spacing, 4/8/12/full corners, search bar, chips, result rows and floating tab bar. What changes is colour, type and one signature detail, so the two sides read as siblings, not twins. Two looks to choose from. "[Sister name]" is a placeholder, and the filters are placeholders until the research report lands. Card art is generic, with no game logos or trademarks.

The app with two sides

One app, both sides, and the same floating tab bar. Two ways to move between them.

Idea 1 · Switch at the top Recommended
The same pill as the website, under the logo. One tap flips the whole app's look. Watchlist and Alerts show both sides with the filter.

Idea 2 · Choose on launch
The first screen asks which side, then the app opens there. Switch later from Account. It's clearer for new users, but slower for people who collect both.

Recommendation

Look

A · Night foil. Foil is what non-sports collectors chase (holos, foils, secret rares), so the signature means something. It's magical through material rather than clip-art. Midnight blue sits opposite F&F's warm black, so the two sides never get confused. B is quieter and more literary. Pick it if A feels too bright.

Switch

A two-part pill beside the logo, in the same place on both sides. It reads as "same company, other side", and it never hides in a menu.

App

Idea 1, the switch at the top. Collectors of both get one tap, and the tab bar stays identical. A first-launch question can come later if the data shows people are confused.

Rules kept

Same layout, spacing, radii and type scale, with one accent family per side. Foil only appears on edges and the top rarity: never full backgrounds and never purple gradients. All copy is plain.
