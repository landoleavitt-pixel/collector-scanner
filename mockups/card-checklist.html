Card checklist

Search a character or an athlete and see every card of them, sorted by set, then card number, then version. Mark the ones you own and get alerts for the ones you're missing. The same page works on both sides. Everything below is clickable: switch between List and Grid, open and close sets, filter by Missing or Owned, and tap any card to mark it.

My recommendation

  1. List view by default, Grid view as an option. The list keeps 80+ cards calm: one line per card number, with its versions as small coloured chips. The grid is for browsing by photo, and it only shows photos when a seller has one listed now.
  2. Progress counts versions, not cards. "You own 9 of 37" means 9 of the 37 versions. Each set also shows its own small progress bar.
  3. One tap on a card opens a short picker (a sheet on phone, a small window on desktop). Tick the versions you own, and use + / − if you have more than one. No prices, no values: this is a "what I own" list, not a portfolio.
  4. "Alert me for the ones I'm missing" becomes one saved search that counts as one of your 5 alerts. It updates itself when you mark a card as owned, so you stop getting alerts for it.
  5. Opens from: a banner at the top of search results ("See every Charizard card"), a link on each result ("Every version of this card"), and a Checklist button on watchlist tiles.
  6. Build order: Pokémon (English) first, then Magic. Fields & Floors uses the card makers' own checklists for each player's card numbers.
Things to know before building.
• Privacy: the cards someone owns are saved to their account. They're only visible to that person, and can be removed. Marking a card needs a free account; anyone can view a checklist without signing in. Magic's catalog rules require free access, and this meets them.
• "For sale now": the counts come from one live eBay search per checklist when the page opens. We don't keep listing history, and we never show "last seen".
• Fields & Floors card lists: each player's card numbers come from the card makers' own checklists (Landon is downloading them). Parallel colours come from our existing rarity lists. The Paul Skenes section below shows real numbers once those files are in.

What "owned" looks like

The ring on the left fills in as you collect a card's versions. Chips for versions you own are filled in with a check mark, and show a count when you have more than one.

04

"Alert me for the ones I'm missing"

One tap opens a short confirmation, then it's saved as a single alert. It shows on the watchlist like any saved search, and it stops alerting for a version as soon as you mark that version owned.

The confirmation. "Only the rarest" is there for people missing 70+ versions who don't want an alert for every common one.

After saving: a short confirmation, and the button changes to "Alerts on".
