Inside the iOS app: no web prices. Base opens Apple's purchase sheet, and the price shown comes from the App Store.

Three treatments

The steps are open on the page background, the free account is a full-width band, and the plans are a single framed group with Base raised. No two neighbouring sections look the same.

No extra length

Same copy and same artifacts. The steps lose their box padding, and on phones the two free plans shrink to one line each. That's about 10% shorter on desktop and about 25% shorter on a phone.

Plan order

Base moves to the middle (desktop) or to the top (phone), so the paid plan is what the eye lands on. The free tiers stay one tap away.

App Store rule

In the app, Plans hides web prices and Stripe, and Base shows the App Store price from RevenueCat. This is gated with isNativeApp(), per Boss's rule.

Tokens only

bg-base, bg-elev and bg-elev-2 with the gold-deep hairlines. The big outlined numerals use a gold-deep stroke. Nothing new to add to globals.css.
