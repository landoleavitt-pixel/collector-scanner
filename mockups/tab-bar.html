Fields & Floors · iOS app · (d)

Bottom tab bar

The app currently uses the website's top menu, which is hard to reach one-handed. A bottom tab bar with Search · Watchlist · Alerts · Account makes it feel like a real iPhone app and keeps the main screens a thumb away. It shows only in the app; the website is unchanged.

OPTION A

Solid bar

The classic iOS pattern, attached to the bottom edge. It's the calmest option and the one people know best.

OPTION B

Floating pill

A rounded bar that floats above the content with a soft blur. It's more premium and editorial, and it echoes the slabbed-card look.

COMPARE

Trade-offs

A · Solid bar

  • Instantly familiar, like Apple's own apps
  • Never covers content
  • Simplest to build: a fixed footer
  • Plainer, less brand character

B · Floating pill

  • Feels premium and matches the slab and grail styling
  • Content scrolls under the blur, so screens feel taller
  • Covers about 70px of content, so lists need bottom padding
  • Slightly more work, and blur costs a little battery on older phones
DETAILS

How it behaves

Four tabs

Search (home), Watchlist (saved cards), Alerts (saved searches) and Account. Guides and Pricing move into Account. The top bar keeps only the seal and wordmark.

Badges

Alerts shows a gold count of new matches since you last looked. Watchlist has no badge, so the bar stays quiet.

Signed out

Watchlist and Alerts still open, but show a one-screen invite (“Save cards you're watching”) with sign-up and sign-in buttons instead of an empty list.

Fits the current setup

The app's web view already stops above the home indicator, so the bar needs no extra safe-area padding, and the launch splash and its seal hand-off are untouched.

App Store rules

The Account tab shows the plan and “Manage membership” (Apple's sheet). No web prices or Stripe, per the existing isNativeApp() gate.

Tokens

Inactive tabs are ink-600 and the active one is gold-bright. The bar uses bg-elev with a line top border (A), or bg-elev-2 at 82% with blur (B). Labels are 11px.
