Still need, and set lists

Five additions to Foils & Fables, shown on a phone first, plus one computer view. The cards you're missing and what's for sale today, whole sets to collect, a quick way to report a wrong card, marking a whole list at once, and an optional shared want list. Photos are stand-ins; cards with no photo show our own card back.

1 · Still need, for sale now

Every version you're missing from the card lists you've started (you've marked at least one card, or turned on an alert), with what's for sale on eBay right now. Most for sale comes first. One alert button covers all of them.

The page. Grouped by card. Each missing version shows a photo when one's for sale, "N for sale" and Find it. The ones not for sale fold away under "6 more not for sale right now".

By set. The same list, grouped by set instead, for people who collect set by set. One tap to switch.

Where you find it. On the signed-in home page, right under search, and at the top of the Lists tab (with a small dot when something new is listed). Lists, marks and this page stay free; only alerts are paid.

2 · Set lists

Collect a whole set, not just one card. Browse sets by game, open a set's checklist ("You have 40 of 102"), see what's for sale per card, and get an alert for the ones you still need from that set.

Browse sets. The card lists page gets a Cards / Sets switch. Sets are listed newest first by game, with how many you have once you've started one.

A set checklist. The same rows as a card list: picture, name, number, rarity, the for-sale line, and your tick. The alert covers the cards you still need from this set.

3 · Wrong card?

If we've labelled a listing as the wrong card or version, one tap tells us. The reason is optional. We only keep the listing, our label and the reason, nothing about you.

In Find it. A small "Wrong card?" under each listing (and in the card popup), quiet enough not to get in the way.

One tap, optional reason. Pick a reason or just send. Done in two taps.

Thanks, we'll check it.

Confirmed. A short thank-you; the listing stays where it is until we've checked.

4 · I have all of these

Marking a full list one card at a time is a chore. One button marks every version on a card list or set list, with an undo in case it was a slip.

The button. Under the progress bar on a card list or set list, beside "Only you can see this".

Marked 12 as yours.Undo

Done, with undo. Every tick fills in, and "Undo" puts them back exactly as they were.

5 · Share my want list

Off unless you turn it on. When it's on, anyone with the link sees only the cards you still need: not your name, your email, or what you own. Turn it off and the link stops working.

The switch. At the bottom of the Still need page. Turning it on shows your link and a Copy button.

What others see. "A collector's want list": only the missing cards, with what's for sale. No owner name anywhere.

On a computer

The Still need page: the list on the left, a summary with the alert and share switch on the right.

Privacy: marks and the Still need page are private. The shared list is opt-in, shows no name or email, and its link stops working when it's turned off. "Wrong card?" keeps only the listing, our label and an optional reason. Limits kept: eBay only, no prices history or value, no game logos or official art.
