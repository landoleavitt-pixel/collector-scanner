Search by team

Two parts. Search a whole team from the search box, or narrow a player's cards to one team. Both run on the listing title: no extra eBay cost.

01

Teams in the search dropdown

Type part of a team name and the team shows up with the players. It has a gold "Team" tag so it stands out. Works for the city or the nickname ("pitt" or "pirates"), and for college teams.

03

Team filter: league first, then team

A new Team section in Filters. Pick a league first, and only that league's teams appear. College adds one step: pick the conference, then the school. Teams that show up in your current results move to the front with a count, so the likely pick is first. One tap on "Any" clears it. It saves with the search and works for alerts.

Three choices for you

  1. Include college teams (about 80 major programs: Duke, Alabama, UConn…), under College → conference → school? (We'd include them.)
  2. Show 8 teams at first with "+ more" to open the rest, or all of a league's teams at once? (We'd show 8, since results-matches come first.)
  3. Same-name teams (Rangers, Kings, Giants, Cardinals, Panthers, Jets): we tell them apart by the city or sport in the title. A title that says only "Rangers" with no city or sport gets left out rather than guessed. OK?
