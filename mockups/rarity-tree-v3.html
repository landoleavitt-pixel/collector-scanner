How Direction 1 meets the clarity rules

  1. 5 seconds: the big line says “Only 25 made”, and the photo on the tree shows where the card sits.
  2. Plain words: “made”, “rarer”, “packs”. No /N without words, no ratios, no jargon.
  3. One idea per line: how many were made, how it ranks, pack odds. Three separate lines.
  4. Key: “Higher on the tree = rarer”, with “Rarest” at the top and “Most common” at the bottom.
  5. One name per branch: only your card's branch is labelled (“Orange Wave · 25 made”). The others are a coloured dot until tapped or hovered. Extra colours at the same number are tiny leaves, not text.
  6. Prominence: the gold branch, the photo and the “This card” tag. The trunk and the other branches use quiet tokens.
  7. About 8 labels on iPhone: three header lines, the key, Rarest, Most common, your branch and the tag.
  8. Fallbacks: one sentence each.

How Direction 2 meets the clarity rules

  1. 5 seconds: follow the gold line from set to card. The big line says “Only 25 made”.
  2. Plain words: the levels read “2 to 25 made” and “Orange · 25 made”. The words Grail, Ultra and so on don't appear.
  3. One idea per line: same three header lines as Direction 1.
  4. Key: rarity here runs sideways, so the key reads “Further left = rarer”. Rule 4's wording is adapted for this layout.
  5. One name per level: only the gold path is labelled, and the alternatives are unlabelled dots.
  6. Prominence: the photo node is the largest item, sitting at the end of the gold line.
  7. About 8 labels on iPhone: three header lines, the key, set, level, colour and the tag.
  8. Fallbacks: one sentence each.
