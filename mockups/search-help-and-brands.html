Spelling help + card company filter

Two additions. Nothing else on the page changes. Shown on desktop and on a phone.

01

Name suggestions while typing

After 2 letters, a short list of matching players drops down under the landing search. Tap one to fill it in. Arrow keys and Enter work on desktop. It matches any part of the name, so "wemb" finds Wembanyama. Works offline, no extra cost.

04

Card company filter

A new section in Filters, under Card Type. Pick one or more companies. After you pick one, its best-known product lines show up underneath. Lines are optional. Pick none and you get every card from that company. Off by default, so normal searches don't change.
