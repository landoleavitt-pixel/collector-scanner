Make it read as made by people

The site already avoids most AI-template looks: real card photos, real eBay data, one colour system, no stock illustrations. What's left are small tells that add up: decorative labels, poetic filler lines, text symbols standing in for icons, glow and motion everywhere, and a few lines that are no longer true. Each fix below is shown before and after. The locked decisions (hero direction A, intro, tokens, type scale, solid gold buttons) are unchanged.

4 · MOTION

One movement at a time

The hero runs four animations at once (float, foil sweep, gold bloom, pulsing dot), plus a fade-up on 28 blocks down the page. Real products move only when something happens. Proposed: the card sits still with a real shadow, the foil sweep plays once when you hover or tap it, and the dot doesn't pulse. Fade-ups stay only on the first screen. Hero direction A (the live card as the hero) is unchanged. This touches the hero, so it's the Landing session's call to build.

5 · GLOW

Gold as an accent, not a light source

A warm gold vignette sits behind every page (globals.css), plus gold glows on the hero, the alert dot and the featured plan. Remove the page-wide vignette and keep gold for things you can press or things that are rare. The paper grain stays: it's subtle and it's what makes the dark feel printed, not generated.
