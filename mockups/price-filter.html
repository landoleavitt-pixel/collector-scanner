A price filter that's easy to use.

Today it's two separate sliders that move in $25 steps up to $5,000, so $15 or $8,000 can't be picked, you can't type an amount, and the minimum can end up above the maximum. Both options below fix that. They're interactive: try them.

What changes either way

  • Type any amount: $15, $8,000, $40,000. There's no $5,000 ceiling.
  • Blank means no limit, shown in plain words ("Any price", "Under $500", "$200 and up").
  • If the minimum is above the maximum, the two swap automatically. No error.
  • Number keypad on phones, big tap targets, and the same look in the filter drawer and the desktop sidebar.
  • Saved searches and share links keep working, and old saved searches open with their prices unchanged.
