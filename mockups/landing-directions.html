Fields & Floors · Landing page mockups

The landing page, made to match the product.

I reviewed the current landing page on iPhone. Below: what holds it back, a reworked intro, and three hero directions. All of them use the exact site colours, fonts and tier chips. Pick one (or mix) and I'll build it.

The best visual is hiddenThe Featured Find card is the most striking thing on the site, and it sits a full screen below the fold.
The first screen is mostly emptyA generic headline and a faint search line, with a quarter of the screen blank above them. Nothing says "rare" or "live."
The intro is disjointed in the appThe app's launch seal goes to black, then a different wordmark fades in while the header is already visible behind it. Three separate beats instead of one motion.
Every section looks the sameEach section is a dark rounded box, so there's no rhythm and nothing stands out on a long scroll.
The plans card is out of dateBase still says "Launching soon · join the waitlist" even though checkout is live.
Intro: one continuous motion

From the seal to the header, in one move.

The iPhone launch screen already shows the seal, so the web intro picks up from that exact frame. The seal lights up, the wordmark rises out of it, and both settle into the header while the hero comes in underneath. About 1.6 seconds instead of 2.6, and it looks the same on the website.

My recommendation

A for the website, plus C's tab bar inside the app.

  • A puts your strongest asset, a real grail card, on the first screen. It's the most premium-feeling option and it's built from content you already have.
  • Inside the iPhone app, a bottom tab bar (Search · Watchlist · Alerts · Account) makes it feel native rather than like a website. That's also our best defence against Apple's "this is just a website" rejection.
  • The new intro ships with either option. The out-of-date "join the waitlist" plans card gets fixed regardless.
