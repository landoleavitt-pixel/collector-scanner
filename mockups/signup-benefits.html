      Rules

      When

      When a signed-out visitor searches and the results arrive. Same trigger for both options. Never on the landing page, during the intro, during the tour, or over a card popup.

      How often

      A: once, then quiet for 7 days after "Not now". B: on each search, until "Not now" for that visit. Signed-in members never see either.

      Tracking

      "Create free account" goes to /signup?src=prompt. The funnel already counts sign-ups by source, so we'll see if it pays off.

      My pick

      Option A on the web and the bottom sheet in the app. It's seen once at the right moment, then leaves people alone. Option B is quieter on screen but repeats on every search, which can start to feel like an ad.
