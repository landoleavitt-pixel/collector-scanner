Notes

Decisions to confirm

Rungs, not rows

One row per print run, with parallels as wrapping chips. Real data: 28 numbered parallels on 14 rungs, and the four /99s share one line. Chips drop the repeated “Refractor”.

Fold around the match

It opens with 2 rungs above and below this card. The folds show a mini colour preview and a count (“5 more common rungs”). One tap opens a fold.

Visible by default

The hero and strip are always on. The ladder is behind one tap so View on eBay stays above the fold on a phone.

Box filter (optional)

Hobby counts Hobby, Jumbo and Breaker boxes. Retail counts Blaster, Monster and Mega. Parallels without odds for that box are dimmed. Optional for v1.

Odds as quiet text

Unnumbered parallels form a chip cloud at the base, with odds in small mono (“1:3 hobby”). Numbered chips skip odds to stay compact.

Type & tokens

An 11px floor, Geist 13 for chips and Geist Mono 13 for runs. Dark tokens and the locked tier chips only. Every swatch gets a light hairline so Black stays visible.
