Type, radius & buttons

The site uses 30+ font sizes today, about 220 of them at 9–11px, which is hard to read in the iPhone app. Below are two scales that cut that to 9 sizes, a 4-step corner radius, and an alternative for the gold button. Nothing changes until you pick.

1 · TYPE SCALE

Two options

They differ only at the small end. A has an 11px floor everywhere. B keeps the tiny tracked uppercase labels at 10px for the editorial look, with an 11px floor for everything else.

4 · THE GOLD BUTTON

Should buttons share the grail colour?

Today the main buttons use the same gold gradient as the Grail chip (/25 and under). On a results page, “Grail” and “click here” look alike.

Keep · grail gradient

What's live now. Rich, but it matches the /25 chip exactly.
