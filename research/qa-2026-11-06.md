# QA pass: live site, 2026-11-06

Full QA of fieldsandfloors.com and /fables on the live site, as a first-time visitor on a phone (375 wide) and on a computer (1440 wide). Nothing was fixed. No accounts were created or signed into; every signed-in flow stopped at the sign-up or sign-in screen.

Screenshots are in `research/qa-2026-11-06/` (file names are given per finding).

**How it was tested**
- A fresh headless Chrome profile, so every visit was a true first visit (tour pops open, no saved searches).
- The built-in browser for real-mouse checks.
- Direct calls to the live search APIs, to check labels the screen doesn't show.
- A crawl of every link on 18 pages.
- Page timing from the browser, plus eBay-backed API timing.
- A text-size, label, alt-text and tap-size audit on each page.
- About 40 eBay-backed API calls.

**Re-tested after the 4:40pm deploy, as asked:** the shared-card star flow signed out, rarity groups, and Magic on the checklist. They are covered in "What changed at 4:40pm" below; the findings use post-deploy behaviour.

Severity: **High** = breaks a first-time visitor's flow or shows wrong facts. **Med** = noticeable, or goes against DECISIONS.md. **Low** = polish.

## Findings, worst first

### 1. High: "Continue with Apple" on the website ends on a raw error page
- **Page and width:** any card's star on both sides, including a shared card link; phone and desktop.
- **Steps:**
  1. Signed out, open `fieldsandfloors.com/?card=v1|820323648122|0` (any live card), or search anything and tap a card's star.
  2. In the "Keep this card" sheet, tap the **top, gold button "Continue with Apple"**.
- **Wrong:** the browser lands on a Supabase address showing only this text: `{"code":400,"error_code":"validation_failed","msg":"Unsupported provider: provider is not enabled"}`. There's no way back except the browser's back button.
- **Why it matters:** it is the primary button in the sheet, and the sign-up and sign-in pages offer Google only on the web.
- **Related, not testable here:** the iPhone app's native Apple sign-in goes through the same Supabase Apple provider, so it will fail the same way until Apple is switched on in Supabase (IOS_APP_SETUP.md section 3, in Landon's to-do).
- **Screenshots:** `apple-web-click.jpg`, `re-apple-web-dead-end.jpg`, `re-share-card-star-tap.jpg`

### 2. High: Foils & Fables never reads a card's grade number, so the Grade filter finds nothing
- **Page and width:** /fables search and the API; both widths.
- **Steps:**
  1. Search Charizard on the Pokémon side and look at graded cards. No card shows a grade chip ("PSA 9" is only in the title).
  2. Open Filters, set Graded, Grader PSA, Grade 10 and search.
  3. Or call `/api/tcg-search?q=Charizard&game=pokemon&graded=graded&grader=PSA&grade=10`.
- **Wrong:**
  - 0 results, though Charizard PSA 10 cards are listed (they appear in the unfiltered list).
  - `grade=9.5` also returns 0.
  - Every listing with a grader and a number in its title has `grade: null` and `gradeLabel: null`: 63 of 63 on the first check, 28 of 28 on the re-check after the deploy.
  - It is the same on all five games (Black Lotus, Luffy, Elsa and Blue-Eyes were checked too).
  - `grader=CGC` still works, so the grader is read and only the number is dropped.
- **Contradicts:** the Nov 1 audit, when `gradeLabel` was populated ("PSA 10"). The other labels (rarity, language) are fine.
- **Likely cause:** the local `parseTitle` reads these titles correctly ("PSA 6" gives `{grader: PSA, grade: 6}`), so the grade is being lost somewhere between parsing and the live response (`lib/tcg/search.js`, where `gradeLabel` is built from `p.grade`). It also breaks the grade step of rarest-first ordering.
- **Screenshots:** `re-fa-results-graded.jpg`

### 3. High: Find it for an original card also returns later reprints, as "exact" matches
- **Page and width:** /fables/find (the "Find it" button on a checklist version); both widths.
- **Steps:** open the Charizard checklist, tap Base Set Charizard No. 4 (Holo), tap "Find it". Same data: `/api/tcg-family-search?game=pokemon&card=charizard&versions=base1:4:h&lang=en`.
- **Wrong:**
  - 9 of the 20 results are the 2026 "30th Celebration Classic Collection" reprint, marked `exact` with the reason "Charizard · Base Set 4/102 · Rare · Holo". These are a different, much cheaper card.
  - Quoted titles with prices:
    - "Charizard 4/102 Pokemon 30th Anniversary Celebration Classic Collection MINT" $155
    - "Pokemon Charizard 30th Celebration 2026 Classic Collection 4/102" $229
    - "POKEMON 4/102 CHARIZARD 30TH CELEBRATION CLASSIC COLLECTION HOLO NM/M" $160.50
    - "Charizard ME: 30th Celebration Classic Collection #4/102 holo Near Mint" $150
  - Next to them sit the real 1999 Base Set cards at $300 to $650.
  - A "Base Set 2 #4 … PSA 8" card ($1,075) is also in the list (flagged only as "3 versions possible").
- **Why it matters:** "Find every version of a card" is the main idea of Foils & Fables.
- **Screenshot:** `find-charizard-base.jpg`

### 4. High: "Save this search" signed out dumps a new visitor on the Sign-in page and loses the search
- **Page and width:** results page on both sides; both widths.
- **Steps:** signed out, search Paul Skenes (or Charizard on /fables), tap **Save this search**.
- **Wrong:**
  - There's no explanation or sign-up sheet. The visitor goes straight to the "Welcome back" Sign-in page (with a "Create account" link at the bottom).
  - The URL is `/login?next=%2F` (sports) or `/login?next=%2Ffables`. After signing up or in, they land on the plain home page with no search and no filters, so the thing they were saving is gone.
  - The star on a card does it better: a sheet saying "Keep this card".
- **Screenshots:** `sv-sports-save.jpg`, `sv-fables-save.jpg`

### 5. Med: Non-holo cards labelled "Holo Rare" in Find it
- **Page and width:** /fables/find; both widths.
- **Steps:** Checklist "Pikachu libre", tap No. 14, tap "Find it" (version Regular).
- **Wrong:**
  - 12 of 23 results are labelled **Holo Rare** (rarity group "Rare"), though the catalog's own rarity for this card is Common and the titles say Non-Holo. Examples:
    - "Pokemon Pikachu Libre (14 - Non-Holo) XY Trainer Kit: Pikachu Libre...[PR-14/30]"
    - "Pikachu Libre 14/30 XY Trainer Kit Non Holo Pokémon"
    - "Pokemon TCG Pikachu Libre 14/30 XY Trainer Non-Holo 2016 English 80 HP"
  - 5 others show Common (right) and 6 show no label. So the same card shows three different rarities.
  - Possible cause: the title-read rarity "Holo Rare" wins when it should defer to the catalog's Common.
- **Screenshot:** `find-pikachu-libre.jpg`

### 6. Med: Black Lotus checklist "Rarest first" puts Alpha, Beta and Unlimited last
- **Page and width:** /fables/checklist?name=Black%20Lotus&game=mtg; both widths.
- **Steps:** open the Black Lotus checklist, tap "Rarest first".
- **Wrong:**
  - The order is Rare (the 2022 and 2023 reprints), then Common (Black Lotus Lounge, Planechase), then Promo, and the Alpha, Beta and Unlimited originals (group **Vintage**) come **last**, below the commons. The most valuable Magic cards land at the bottom of "Rarest first".
  - The results ranking treats Vintage as rare (a Vintage-first order in the earlier audit).
  - "Black Lotus Lounge" is a different card but is listed as a Black Lotus version.
- **Screenshot:** `re-ck-black-lotus-rarest.jpg`

### 11. Med: Slowest pages
- **Search:**
  - The first sports search took 4.2 to 5.1 s on the phone profile (`/api/search`).
  - The Foils & Fables home "Rarest right now" strip waits 5.4 to 5.7 s (`/api/tcg-search?priceMin=100`) before any card appears, and a first Charizard search took 2.6 to 3.5 s.
  - On the sports home, `/api/rarest` took 4.1 s and `/api/featured` 1.9 s in the built-in browser.
  - Repeat searches inside the hour are fast.

- **Bigger one:** the Foils & Fables home looks empty for ~5 s on a first visit (placeholders, then cards).

## What changed at 4:40pm (re-test)

- **Shared-card star flow:** works. `?card=…` opens the popup, tapping Watch opens "Keep this card" over it, and Use email goes to `/signup?next=…&src=star` and keeps the card in `next`. The gold **Continue with Apple** is the broken part (#1). The "View on eBay" button is now a full-width button (fixed since last week).
- **Rarity groups:** live and good on screen. Results show "Extremely rare / Mega Hyper Rare"; the filter sheet shows Extremely rare, Very rare, Rare, Uncommon, Common, Promo and Vintage with plain descriptions ("Fewer than one in a box of packs"). The group filter works (Pikachu + Extremely rare returns only Secret and Hyper Rare cards; Black Lotus + Vintage returns Vintage). Problems are #5 (Holo Rare on Common cards) and #6 (Vintage order).
- **Magic on the checklist:** works. Black Lotus (15), Sheoldred (64), Lightning Bolt (102) and "Lotus" (187) load in 2.2 to 2.5 s, grouped by set with rarity labels and the Scryfall credit line. Newest-sets view hides the oldest sets behind "Show N older sets", which keeps Alpha and Beta out of sight at first.

## What passed

- **Search:** both sides, results, Sort (sheet on phones, drop-down on desktop), "Load 200 more" and "Show more", price quick picks and boxes (the max box is no longer cut off at 375), More filters, free shipping (644 → 300 results), the five Foils & Fables filters (holo, reverse holo, alternate art, promo only and hide, raw condition, free shipping, accepts offers; all return what they say), and the "Keep this card" sheet.
- **Checklist:** Charizard (130 versions), Pikachu Libre (2), Sheoldred, Black Lotus; tapping a row opens a big photo and Find it, with "Make a free account to mark the cards you have."
- **Find it:** works for a tiny family (24 for sale) and for Charizard Base Set (10 to 20), apart from #3 and #5.
- **Pages:** sign-up and login look right at 375 (no double logo now); Privacy, Terms, Alerts, Filters, How it works, Guides, Glossary, Reading list and Contact all load.
- **Links:** 22 internal links and 13 external links checked; none dead. Five reading-list links to 130point, Card Ladder and PSA return 403 to automated checks (they block bots; they open in a normal browser).
- **`/choose` returns 404 on fieldsandfloors.com by design:** the chooser belongs to floorsandfables.com's domain rewrite in `middleware.js`.
