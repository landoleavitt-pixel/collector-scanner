# QA pass: live site, 2026-10-31

Read-only check of fieldsandfloors.com and fieldsandfloors.com/fables, signed out, at phone width (375) and desktop width (1440). No code was changed and nothing was signed into or created.

Screenshots are in `research/qa-2026-10-31/`. Timed headless-Chrome shots are in `research/qa-2026-10-31/headless/`.

**How it was tested:** the built-in browser at 375 and 1440. A headless Chrome run with timed screenshots re-checked anything timing-related, because the browser pane was hidden part of the time, which slows animations. Three "blank screen / layers on top of each other" problems I first saw were caused by that, and are **not** reported below.

**Not reported, as asked:** the missing refresh popup and the spelling suggestions on Foils & Fables (the design agent is fixing those).

**All clear:**
- No console errors on any page.
- No dead links: every header and footer link returns 200.
- Home and search fast in headless Chrome (first paint 0.1 to 1.2 s).
- Sign-up prompt appears after the 4th card on both sides.
- Sort, "Load 200 more", the tour (6 steps), share links and the side switch all work.
- No text under 11px, apart from the 7.5px "F&F" inside the round logo.
- No page-wide sideways scrolling, except the filter sheet (issue 6).

Severity: **High** = visibly wrong, misleading, or breaks a main flow. **Med** = noticeable, or goes against DECISIONS.md. **Low** = polish.

---

## High

### 1. Foils & Fables: any link with an extra parameter opens a results list, not the home page
- **Page / width:** /fables, both widths
- **Steps:** open `fieldsandfloors.com/fables?utm_source=test` (or `/fables?internal=1`).
- **Wrong:** instead of the home page ("Chase the shine." plus search), it opens a search results page for "All games" (757 listings). Any ad, email or social link with a tracking tag lands people there. `?internal=1`, the flag agents use to stay out of funnel numbers, does the same. Fields & Floors with the same parameters shows its home page normally.
- **Screenshots:** `23-fables-param-opens-all-games-results-375.jpg`, `headless/fables-utm-375-6000ms.jpg`

### 2. Recent search "Pittsburgh Pirates" shows as "Pittsburgh Perales" and finds nothing
- **Page / width:** Fields & Floors home ("Pick up where you left off"), both widths
- **Steps:** search for Pittsburgh Pirates (or open a shared Pirates link), then go back to the home page and tap the recent-search chip.
- **Wrong:** the chip reads "Pittsburgh Perales" and runs that search: 0 listings, "No results found". The stored search is correct ("Pittsburgh Pirates"); only the display and re-run are wrong.
- **Likely cause:** `tidyRecentSearches` in `lib/nameMatcher.js` runs spelling correction on every recent search, including team names. The search box itself skips team names (`teamFromQuery` check in `app/page.js`); this list doesn't.
- **Screenshots:** `20-recent-chip-pittsburgh-perales-1440.jpg`, `21-perales-search-0-results-1440.jpg`

### 3. Foils & Fables results heading squashed on phones
- **Page / width:** /fables search results, 375
- **Steps:** on a phone, search "Charizard".
- **Wrong:** the heading shows only "C." (or "A." for All games), and "695 listings, ranked by the rarity printed on the card" wraps one word per line down the left edge. "Save this search" and "New search" take the rest of the row. Fine at desktop width.
- **Screenshots:** `15-fables-results-heading-squashed-375.jpg`, `headless/fables-375-2500ms.jpg`

### 4. The "Live right now" featured card on the home page is a fan-made custom item
- **Page / width:** Fields & Floors home, 375 (desktop showed a different, real card at the time)
- **Steps:** open the home page signed out and look at "Live right now".
- **Wrong:** the featured find was "Custom 1/1 Shohei Ohtani Jersey, /25 Auto And Gem 10 Patch Card Raree Dispay", $35,000, tagged /25 and AUTO. It's a framed custom display, not a card. It undercuts the "professionally made, real data" decision and the Grail pitch. Search results let in custom items too (issue 7).
- **Screenshot:** `01-home-custom-card-375.jpg`

### 5. Foils & Fables cards go straight to eBay: no card popup, so no "search tool only" line
- **Page / width:** /fables results, both widths
- **Steps:** search "Charizard" and tap a card.
- **Wrong:** every result is a direct link to eBay in a new tab. On Fields & Floors, a tap opens the card popup with photos, rarity, Watch and Share, and the short "Sold by eBay sellers, not us. Labels are read from listing titles and can be wrong." line. DECISIONS asks for that line on the card popup, and says Foils & Fables "works like Fields & Floors".
- **Screenshot:** `18-fables-cards-link-straight-to-ebay-375.jpg`

## Med

### 6. Filter sheet: price "max" box cut off on phones, sheet scrolls sideways
- **Page / width:** Fields & Floors search, Filters sheet, 375
- **Steps:** search a player, tap Filters, scroll to Price range.
- **Wrong:** the "to $ No max" box runs off the right edge (the sheet's content is 555px wide in a 375px screen). A sideways scroll bar shows at the bottom of the sheet the whole time.
- **Screenshots:** `04-price-max-cut-off-375.jpg`, `05-filter-sheet-sideways-scroll-375.jpg`

### 7. Wrong rarity and auto labels on real results
- **Page / width:** Fields & Floors, search "Paul Skenes" (Numbered), both widths
- **Wrong:**
  - "Paul Skenes (1 of 1,060 made) ~ 2023 Choice Bradenton Marauders" shows as **/1**, Grail gold. It's 1 of 1,060.
  - "2023 Leaf Metal Pre-Production Proof **Unsigned** 1/1" is tagged **AUTO**.
  - Custom cards ("… Built By Patch Lab", from the seller patchlabcardartcreations) sit in the top /1 results.
- **Screenshot:** `03-1-of-1060-as-1of1-375.jpg`

### 8. eBay claims that aren't strictly accurate
- **Page / width:** tour step 2, How it works, footer; all widths
- **Wrong:** DECISIONS asks for claims about eBay we can back up. eBay's Advanced Search and boolean search can combine these filters, and eBay has a "Print Run" item specific:
  - **Tour step 2:** "Autographed, rookie, numbered to 25 or lower, under $300, all at once. eBay can't combine those. We can."
  - **How it works:**
    - "eBay has no concept of a print run."
    - "you can't tell eBay: autographed, rookie, numbered to 25 or lower, under $300…"
    - "We combine filters eBay won't."
  - **Footer:** "Search every numbered sports card on eBay" (every is more than we can promise).
- **Screenshot:** `12-tour-step2-ebay-claim-375.jpg`

### 9. Foils & Fables doesn't work like Fields & Floors in several places
- **Page / width:** /fables, both widths
- **Differences (DECISIONS: "Same interactions everywhere… only colours, fonts and game-specific filters differ"):**

| | Fields & Floors | Foils & Fables |
|---|---|---|
| Filters | bottom sheet on phones, side panel on desktop, "Edit filters" and active-filter chips under the heading | panel that opens inline above results; no active-filter chips |
| Price | quick picks (Under $50 … No max) plus min/max boxes | min/max boxes only |
| Sort | Sort sheet on phones | browser drop-down |
| Share | "Share" button on results and in the card popup | no Share anywhere |
| Card tap | card popup | straight to eBay (issue 5) |
| Page size | "Load 200 more" | 48 at a time, "Show more" |
| Footer links | sports pages | the same sports pages: How it works talks about players and print runs, Site tour types "Cooper Flagg" |
| Header (desktop) | How it works link | no How it works link |

- **Screenshots:** `17-fables-filters-inline-no-price-picks-375.jpg`, `headless/fables-search-1440-7000ms.jpg`
