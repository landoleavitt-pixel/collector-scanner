# Pre-push QA: what's waiting on main, 2026-11-07

**What was tested:** a scratch production build of HEAD `ea851f5`, run on its own port (never built in the main folder), as a first-time visitor. About 45 eBay-backed calls in all.
- **Phone (375) and computer (1440):** headless Chrome with a fresh profile each time, plus direct API calls.
- **Last week's report:** every finding in `research/qa-2026-11-06.md` was re-checked.
- **New Foils & Fables features:** the home, versions-first search, the unsure page, four games, the set filter, the "Save this search" sheet and Vintage.
- **Wrong cards:** I opened a sample of "this card" rows on every game and read the listings, to catch wrong cards.
- **Side colours:** the 748bbe1 colours were checked with fake data on a scratch-only page.
- **iPhone Simulator:** a brief check.

No code changed, no accounts were created, and nothing was signed into. Screenshots are in `research/qa-2026-11-07/`.

Severity: **High** = shows wrong cards or untrue numbers, or breaks a main flow. **Med** = noticeable. **Low** = polish.

## Findings, worst first

### 1. High: "Pick your card" and multi-card listings are attached to one specific card
- **Where:** versions-first Pikachu, rows No. 025, 026 and 051 of "30th Celebration"; Find it for each row; phone and computer.
- **Steps:** search Pikachu, open the "Pikachu No. 025" row (then 026, 051).
- **Wrong:** 9 of the 10 listings under each of these three rows are the same generic multi-card listings. For a shopper they are not that card:
  - "30th Celebration Pokemon Cards Pick Your Pikachu Singles"
  - "Pokemon 30th Anniversary Cards Reverse Holo Pikachu Rare EX Master Set Singles"
  - "Pokémon 30th Celebration: Pick Your Card – Holo/Pikachu Rare/ex Near Mint TCG"
  - "Pokémon Trading Card - 30th Celebration Pikachu Rare - Choose Your Card"
- **Also:** the Pikachu row "No. BW54 · Regular · Common" shows "Pikachu 054/128 Double Rare 30th Anniversary Celebration Pokemon TCG", which is a different card from a different set.
- **Why it matters:** the home page promises "Only the real card".

### 2. High: A version's Find it page mixes in other rarities of the same card
- **Where:** Dark Magician, row "the Pharaoh's Servant No. MAMO-EN001 · Grand Master Rare" (labelled Extremely rare); both widths.
- **Wrong:** the row says "1 for sale"; its Find it page says "69 for sale on eBay now". Only the first is a Grand Master Rare:
  - "PSA 10 Yu-Gi-Oh! MAMO Dark Magician the Pharaoh's Servant GMR SERIALIZED 80/100" is right.
  - "Dark Magician, the Pharaoh's Servant Yugioh MAMO-EN001 Overframe Starlight 1st" is a different rarity.
  - "Konami Yu-Gi-Oh! Dark Magician, the Pharaoh's Servant MAMO-EN001 Ultra 1st Ed" is a different rarity.
  - "PSA 9 EXTENDED ART-PRISMATIC SECRET RARE DARK MAGICIAN THE PHARAOH'S SERVANT" is a different rarity.
- **Similar:** Sheoldred's Edict No. 97 "Regular…" also lists "FOIL SHEOLDRED'S EDICT SECRETS OF STRIXHAVEN…" and "1x Sheoldred's Edict Foil…". The foils belong to a different version.
- **Screenshots:** `n10-dark-magician.jpg`

### 3. High: Sealed packs, lots and other products get through the "only the real card" filter
- **Where:** Find it and the unsure page, Pokémon; both widths.
- **Wrong, quoted exactly:**
  - Umbreon ex No. 092 (30th Celebration, Regular) lists "(SEALED) Espeon ex Day #108 & Umbreon ex Night #110 Pokemon 30th Celebration". That is a sealed two-card pack, and a different card number.
  - The Charizard unsure page ("15 for sale") lists:
    - "Pokémon Special Delivery Charizard Sealed Pokemon Center Promo Sealed Cello Wrap"
    - "Pokemon TCG Assorted Cards - EX/GX/V/VMAX/VSTAR/CHARIZARD - MINT CARDS!"
    - "Pokemon Topps Chrome Charizard #06 - NM" (a Topps product, not a TCG card)
- **Screenshots:** `n20-unsure-page.jpg`

### 4. High: The "N for sale" numbers aren't true or steady
- **Where:** versions-first lists (Charizard, Pikachu, Elsa, Dark Magician, Umbreon); both widths.
- **Row count vs the page it opens** (all on HEAD):

  | Row | Row says | Its Find it page says |
  |---|---|---|
  | Mega Charizard Y ex No. 022 | 3 | 82 |
  | Elsa - Ice Artisan No. 123 | 1 | 108 |
  | Dark Magician the Pharaoh's Servant MAMO-EN001 | 1 | 69 |
  | Umbreon ex No. 092 | 12 | 36 |
  | Pikachu No. 025 | 1 | 10 |
  | Charizard No. 4 (Vintage) | 6 | 8 |
  | Charizard No. 25 | 1 | 2 |

  - Black Lotus rows were right (19, 24, 9, 11 matched).
- **Header numbers change between visits:** Charizard read "130 versions, 95+ for sale / We hid 72 listings" on one load and "130 versions, 123+ for sale / We hid 47" on another. Row No. 001 went from "2 for sale" to "10 for sale".
- **Wording:** "130 versions, 95+ for sale" reads as 95 versions for sale. The number is listings, and includes the 15 "unsure" ones.
- **Screenshots:** `n05-search-result-top.jpg`, `n07-row-opened.jpg`

### 5. Med: Saved searches on Foils & Fables say "NO FILTERS" even when they have filters
- **Where:** saved-search rows (`WatchlistRow.js`); tested on a scratch-only fake-data page.
- **Wrong:** a Foils & Fables saved search with Game = Pokémon, Rarity = Extremely rare and Graded shows the grey chip "NO FILTERS". The row's `buildChips` only knows the sports filters (print runs, auto, rookie…); it never reads the Foils & Fables ones (game, rarity, graded, grade, set, finish, art…). The alert itself still uses them.
- **Colours (748bbe1) work:**
  - **Rows:** a Foils & Fables row gets a cyan border, glow and toggle, and a Sports row stays gold.
  - **Starred-card tiles:** the Foils & Fables tile gets a cyan price, chip and star, and the sports tile stays gold.
- **Small:** in a two-column grid at 375, the tile's price "$250.00" and the "VIEW" button touch.
- **Screenshots:** `n40-fake-colours.jpg`

### 6. Med: The iPhone app's Foils & Fables side opens with the sports tour
- **Where:** iPhone Simulator (iPhone 18 Pro, iOS 27), Foils & Fables tile on the chooser.
- **Wrong:** the first-visit tour shows in Night foil colours, but the words are the sports ones: "Start with a player. Type any name. We only show listings where that player is actually in the title, so no keyword-stuffed junk," with "Cooper Flagg" in the box. A card-game visitor's first screen talks about players.

### 7. Med: Base Set Charizard still shows reprints (last week's #3 only partly fixed)
- **Where:** Find it for Base Set Charizard No. 4 (Holo).
- **Wrong:** 3 of 8 results are the 2026 reprint, written with other wording than the filter catches: "Charizard 4/102 30th Anniversary", "Charizard (4/102) Holofoil from the Pokémon 30th Anniversary Celebration" and "Charizard 4/102 30th Celebrations Holo Rare 2026 English". The "Classic Collection" wording is now filtered.

### 8. Med: The app couldn't run on the iOS 17.5 simulator
- **Where:** `xcodebuild` of `ios/App` (Debug, simulator, no code signing change), then launch.
- **What happened:** the build succeeds. On the iOS 17.5 simulator the app closes at once with "Library not loaded: SwiftUICore.framework" (`App.debug.dylib`). On iOS 27 it runs fine.
- **Why it matters:** the project says minimum iOS 15. This may only be a debug-build effect, but it should be proven on a release or archive build on an iOS 17 device or simulator before submitting.

### 9. Med: The app asks for notification permission right after sign-in, with no explanation
- **Where:** `NativeBridge.js`.
- **Wrong:** once someone signs in, every user is asked for push at once ("Early access: everyone is asked"). The app shows no message first. `research/app-review-risks.md` advises asking when someone turns on an alert, so the reviewer sees why.

### 10. Low: A first-time Foils & Fables visitor is left guessing in a few places
- **"Only what you're missing":** the home says "We tell you when one you need is listed" but never says you mark the cards you own (the checklist), so "need" isn't explained.
- **"Version":** the word is never defined on the home, and the search box just says "Which card?" with no example.
- **One Piece:** the home lists four games, but searching Luffy (One Piece) silently gives the old list ("Luffy cards … All cards"), not the versions view.
- **Branding on the way to sign-up:** from "Alert me when one I need is listed" on Foils & Fables you land on a sign-up page in the sports gold look, with "Fields & Floors" in the header and tabs, and no cyan.
- **Good:** the new home reads clearly in a glance ("Every version of a card. One search."), and the three icon lines are plain.
- **Screenshots:** `n02-fables-home-8s.jpg`, `n21-alert-me.jpg`, `n10-luffy.jpg`

### 11. Low: Fewer labels
- Find it for Pikachu Libre No. 14: 16 of 21 listings show no rarity label at all (earlier 5 Common, 16 none).
- Chips under the card name sometimes show a group ("Very rare") next to the specific name ("Double Rare"); fine, but wordy on a phone.

### 12. Low: Small tap targets and speed
- Footer links on Foils & Fables (Support 43×18, Privacy 40×18, Terms 33×18) are well under 44 px tall.
- The Foils & Fables home "For sale now on eBay" strip took 6.3 s on a cold server, with nothing shown until it arrives.

## Warming /api/rarest and /api/featured (item 3): not done, described
- **Why it can't be done without Landon:**
  - There's no `vercel.json` in the project and the site is on Vercel's free plan, whose built-in timers can only run once a day, so an hourly warm-up from Vercel needs Pro (which is a launch-day step).
  - The only hourly timer is the Supabase one that runs the alert checker.
- **Cheapest options:**
  1. At launch, with Vercel Pro, add a `vercel.json` hourly cron that calls both addresses.
  2. Or, with no new accounts: have the alert checker's hourly run (`supabase/functions/poller`) fetch `/api/rarest` and `/api/featured` once at the end. It ships with the poller deploy Landon is already doing. It would warm the CDN copy in one region only, so it helps but isn't a guarantee.
- **Measured:** first visitor each hour waits about 4 s for `/api/rarest` and 2 s for `/api/featured`; the rest are 0.16 to 0.3 s.
