# Name check: "Floors & Fables"

*Done 2026-11-06. Research only; no code changed. **This is a first look, not legal advice or a trademark clearance.** It tells you whether anything obvious is in the way. It can't tell you the name is safe.*

## The short version

**I found nothing in the way of "Floors & Fables."** No exact match and no close match turned up in any of the five places I could check. Nothing I found changes the name decision.

| Where I looked | Result |
|---|---|
| **Apple App Store** (US and Canada, public search) | **Clear.** No app called "Floors & Fables", "Floors and Fables" or a close variant |
| **US trademarks (USPTO)** | **Clear.** No mark contains both "floors" and "fables" |
| **Canadian trademarks (CIPO)** | **Clear.** The search for both words returned **0** results |
| **The web** (cards, games, flooring, podcasts) | **Clear.** No business, product or title with this name |
| **Domains** | **Still available, so not yet bought:** floorsandfables.com and floorsandfables.ca (and foilsandfables.com and .ca) were all unregistered on 2026-11-06 |

**Two things worth doing now (cheap, and they hold the name):**
1. **Register the domains.** All four were still free this morning.
2. **Create the app record "Floors & Fables" in App Store Connect.** Apple only confirms a name is free when the record is created, and it's first come, first served.

**Not covered here:** Google Play, social-media handles, trademark offices outside the US and Canada, and pending applications filed in the last few days. Details below.

## 2. US trademarks (USPTO)

**How:** the USPTO's public Trademark Search, searching "floors fables" and then reading the results by hand.

**What the search does:** it returns marks containing *either* word, so I pulled the results and looked for any mark with both words. It reported 1,029 results and I captured 944 of them (a few were lost when moving between pages), so this is a thorough sample rather than a perfect list.

**Findings:**
- **No mark contains both "FLOOR(S)" and "FABLE(S)".** No exact match, no near match.
- **"FABLES" is a crowded word.** 110 of the marks I captured contain it, and about 30 are live in entertainment, software, books and card or board games. The nearest in our field:

| Mark | Status | What it covers | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| WACKY FABLES | Registered | **Card games** (class 28) | Think2Master LLC |
| THREADED FABLES | Pending | Downloadable games and board games (classes 9, 28) | Stonemaier LLC |
| MERGE FABLES | Registered | Downloadable game apps (class 9) | Microfun Limited |
| MYSTIC FABLES | Pending | Video game software (classes 9, 41) | an individual |
| FOOD FABLES | Pending | Downloadable game software (class 9) | Cobalt Moon Studios |
| HAUS OF FABLES | Registered | Advertising and PR services (class 35) | an individual |

  None starts with "Floors," none is about searching for cards, and each has a different first word. A crowded field also means **"Fables" alone is a weak, shared word**: a big part of the name's distinctiveness comes from "Floors & Fables" as a whole.
- **"FLOORS" marks are all flooring:** about 97 live marks containing "floor(s)" in classes 9, 28, 35, 41 and 42. They cover flooring retail, wholesale, installation and coatings (for example FLOORS REVOLUTION, FLOORS TO GO, SHAW FLOORS). None involves games, cards or search tools.

**Limits:** the USPTO search doesn't catch unregistered ("common-law") uses, applications filed very recently, or sound-alike marks that don't share spelling.

## 4. The web

**How:** web searches for the exact name and close variants, together with words for flooring, card games, board games and podcasts.

- **"Floors & Fables" / "Floors and Fables":** no business, product, show, game or brand with this name. The only "Floors" title that came up is Patrick Carman's children's novel *Floors* (Scholastic), which has no connection to "Fables".
- **"Floors and Fables" flooring company:** none found. Flooring results were ordinary flooring businesses (Floors Direct, Floors Etc., Abbey Flooring).
- **"Floor Fables", "Fable Floors", "Fables Flooring":** nothing for cards, board games or flooring. Only flooring-industry podcasts came up, none with this name.

**Limits:** a web search can't prove a negative. A small local flooring shop, a new podcast or a social-media account with this name might not be indexed. I did not search Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Etsy or Google Play, which all need sign-in or block automated visits, and I didn't try to get past that.

## 6. What I could not check

- **Google Play, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Etsy and other handles:** need sign-in or block automated visits.
- **Trademarks outside the US and Canada** (for example the EU, UK): not searched. If the app is sold outside North America, they matter.
- **Sound-alike names** (for example "Floor & Fable", "Floors n Fables"): the databases I used match spelling, not sound. The closest spellings I tried found nothing.
- **Common-law rights:** a business using the name without registering it can still object. A web search is the only check, and it found none.
- **Brand-new filings:** filings from the last few days may not be in either database yet.
- **Whether "FLOORS" for flooring could object to a card tool called "Floors":** I found nothing that suggests it. This is a legal judgement, not something a search can settle.

## 7. What this means for the name decision

- **Nothing found changes the decision.** The name is clear in every place I could check.
- **Landon's earlier decision stands** (no trademark lawyer before launch, made knowing this is a first look). If you ever want more certainty, the standard next step is a professional clearance search covering sound-alikes and other countries. I'm not recommending it as a blocker.
- **Same family names:** the side names "Fields & Floors" and "Foils & Fables" were checked in earlier research (research/non-sports-filters.md section 6 and the earlier name check in APP_STORE_LISTING.md). "Fields & Fables" is taken as a domain.
